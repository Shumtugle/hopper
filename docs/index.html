Hopper

A puzzle for Android, seen from inside a drop of quicksilver. The drop never stops bouncing; all you choose is where the next bounce goes.

tap the black

This is a level of the game itself: its islands, grown by the game's code, and its liquid, lit by the game's own shaders. Tap to raise it again; drag to walk round it.

In the level

  • Shards glow in the pits of the islands. Take them all and the gate opens.
  • Glass holds while you bounce on it, and sinks the moment you leave.
  • Springs throw you two cells along their glowing arrow, and a floor up. They are the only way up.
  • Floors float one over another. Under a floor you can hop, but not leap: the floor is a ceiling.
  • Hot plates burn, unless you carry a silver bead to cool one.
  • Mirages look like islands and hold nothing. On each, a reflection of the drop bounces in step with you.
  • Some levels shift. After a number of bounces the ground trembles, then everything but the island under you goes down into the liquid and a new act rises around you. Shards you did not reach move on with you.

Ten levels are drawn by hand; after them, every level is grown from its own number and checked by a solver, so there is always a next one.

The watcher and the hopscotch

Beside the map sits the watcher, an eye in a stepped frame of bismuth. Tap it, and it plays the level for you, turning and hopping as a finger would; swipe, and the drop is yours again. After its game comes the hopscotch: every level as a chalked square, numbers climbing up the screen without end. Any level played can be played again.

Controls

  • Swipe up: the next bounce goes one cell ahead. A long swipe leaps over a cell.
  • Swipe up and aside: one cell across a corner.
  • Swipe sideways to turn, down to turn round. Tap to look at the map.
  • Tap the eye to let the watcher play. Back opens the hopscotch.
